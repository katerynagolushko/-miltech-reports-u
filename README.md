# Ukrainian Defense Tech Intelligence

Comprehensive analysis from the UA Online Miltech Conference 2026.

## 8 Research Reports

1. **State of the Ukrainian Defence Market** - How Ukraine became the world's largest defense tech testing ground
2. **Funding and Deals in Defence Tech** - From Brave1 grants to €100M joint ventures
3. **Building the Right Products for the Front** - Field-driven product development
4. **AI on the Battlefield** - What Ukraine builds vs. what NATO buys
5. **Selling to the Ukrainian State** - Real stakeholders, decisions, and risks
6. **Exporting Ukrainian Defence Tech** ⭐ - Ukraine vs. West comparison matrix (FLAGSHIP)
7. **Hiring and Keeping Defence Talent** - Retention through impact
8. **Ukrainian Tax, Law and Staff Mobilization** - Defence City vs. Diia City
